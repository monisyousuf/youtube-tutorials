# Onboarding Guide

## Equipment Setup

Your laptop will be shipped to your home address or available for collection at the office on your first day, depending on the arrangement confirmed in your welcome email. All machines come pre-enrolled in our Mobile Device Management system and include the standard software bundle: Slack, Zoom, Chrome, VS Code, and the company VPN client. On first boot, follow the setup wizard to connect to your company Google Workspace account using your assigned email address in the format firstname.lastname@company.com. Once logged in, open the IT Self-Service portal from your browser bookmarks to install any additional tools required for your role. Hardware accessories such as an external monitor, keyboard, and mouse can be requested through the IT portal under the Equipment Request section. Delivery takes three to five business days. If you encounter any hardware issues in your first week, contact IT support directly via the #it-help Slack channel or email it-support@company.com. Do not attempt to reinstall the operating system or remove the MDM profile, as this will lock your device.

## System Access

Access to core systems is provisioned automatically on your start date based on your role. You will receive a Slack invite, Google Workspace account, and access to the project management tool within the first hour of your start time. Access to additional systems such as the code repository, cloud infrastructure dashboards, and customer data platforms must be requested through your manager using the Access Request form in the IT portal. Your manager should submit these requests at least two business days before your start date to avoid delays. You will receive a welcome email from each platform with instructions for setting your password. All accounts must use a unique, strong password and be enrolled in multi-factor authentication within twenty-four hours. The company password manager, 1Password, is available to all employees and its use is strongly encouraged. A shared team vault for non-sensitive credentials will be shared with you by your manager.

## First Week Schedule

Your first week follows a structured programme designed to help you get productive quickly without feeling overwhelmed. Day one focuses on equipment setup, system access, and a one-hour welcome call with your manager. Days two and three are dedicated to team introductions, reviewing internal documentation in Notion, and attending any standing team meetings as an observer. Days four and five include role-specific onboarding sessions arranged by your manager, which may include shadowing a colleague, completing required compliance training modules, or reviewing the current project roadmap. By the end of week one, you should have completed your IT setup checklist, finished the mandatory security awareness training, scheduled a thirty-minute coffee chat with at least three colleagues, and had your first one-on-one with your manager. The full onboarding checklist is available in Notion under the New Hires space.

## Key Contacts

Your primary contact for day-to-day questions is your line manager, whose details appear in your offer letter and Google directory. For HR-related matters including payroll, benefits enrolment, and leave requests, contact the People Operations team at people@company.com or via the #people-ops Slack channel. IT support is available Monday to Friday, nine to six, via #it-help on Slack or it-support@company.com, with a target response time of four hours for non-urgent issues. Your onboarding buddy, assigned before your start date, is a peer volunteer who can answer informal questions about team culture, processes, and company life. Their name and Slack handle will be in your welcome email. For questions about your equity grant or compensation, reach out to equity@company.com. Emergency contacts and the employee assistance programme details are listed in the HR section of the Notion wiki.
