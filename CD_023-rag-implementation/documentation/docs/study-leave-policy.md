# Study Leave Policy

## Eligibility Criteria

All permanent employees who have completed at least twelve months of continuous service are eligible to apply for study leave. The policy covers formal education programs at accredited institutions, including undergraduate and postgraduate degrees, professional certifications, and vocational qualifications directly relevant to your role or a role within the company. Employees on fixed-term contracts are eligible on a pro-rata basis after six months of service. Part-time employees may apply and their entitlement will be calculated proportionally based on their contracted hours. Study leave is not available for informal online courses, self-paced learning platforms, or programs that have not been approved in advance by your line manager and the HR department. Employees who have previously taken study leave must wait a minimum of two years before applying again, unless the new application is for a continuation of the same program. Approval is at the discretion of HR and your department head, taking into account business needs and team capacity at the time of application.

## How to Apply

To apply for study leave, complete the Study Leave Request Form available on the HR portal and submit it at least eight weeks before the intended start date. The form requires you to include the name of the institution, the course title, the expected duration, the examination or submission schedule, and a brief statement explaining how the program relates to your current role or career development within the company. Your line manager must sign the form before it is submitted to HR. HR will review the application within ten business days and may request a meeting to discuss the proposal further. If approved, you will receive a formal confirmation letter outlining the terms, including any cost-sharing arrangement. If your application is declined, HR will provide written reasons and you may reapply after addressing the stated concerns.

## Duration and Pay During Leave

Study leave may be granted for periods ranging from a single day for an examination to a continuous block of up to three months for intensive coursework or a dissertation period. For the first four weeks of approved study leave, employees receive full basic salary. From week five onwards, pay is reduced to fifty percent of basic salary. Any period beyond three months is unpaid. The company will not cover tuition fees as part of the study leave benefit, but employees may apply separately to the Learning and Development budget for a tuition contribution of up to two thousand pounds per academic year, subject to availability. Study leave days cannot be combined with annual leave to extend a paid period beyond the limits above. Public holidays falling within a study leave period count as public holidays, not study leave days.

## Returning to Work

Upon completion of the approved study period, you are expected to return to your substantive role. You must notify HR and your line manager at least two weeks before your planned return date. Within thirty days of returning, you are required to submit a short report to HR summarising what you studied and how you intend to apply the learning in your role. Failure to return without a documented reason may result in repayment of salary received during the leave period. If you completed a qualification with company support and leave the organisation within twelve months of returning, you may be asked to repay up to fifty percent of any tuition contribution received. HR will discuss a repayment schedule in that situation.
