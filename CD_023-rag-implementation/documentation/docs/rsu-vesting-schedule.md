# RSU Vesting Schedule

## What Are RSUs

Restricted Stock Units, or RSUs, are a form of equity compensation granted to employees as part of their total compensation package. Unlike stock options, RSUs do not require you to pay an exercise price. Instead, each RSU represents a promise by the company to deliver one share of common stock to you at a future date, provided you remain employed and meet the vesting conditions. RSUs are granted in units and have no monetary value at the time of grant because you do not own the shares until they vest. Once an RSU vests, the shares are transferred to your brokerage account and become ordinary taxable income based on the fair market value of the shares on the vesting date. The company withholds a portion of vesting shares to cover your income tax liability, a process known as sell-to-cover. RSUs are disclosed in your offer letter or compensation statement under the equity section and are administered through the company's equity platform, currently Carta.

## Vesting Schedule

All RSU grants follow a four-year vesting schedule with a one-year cliff. This means that no shares vest during the first twelve months after your grant date. At the one-year anniversary of your grant date, twenty-five percent of your total grant vests in a single tranche. After the cliff, the remaining seventy-five percent vests quarterly in equal instalments over the following three years, resulting in twelve additional quarterly vestings of approximately 6.25 percent each. For example, if you received a grant of four thousand RSUs on the first of January, one thousand units would vest on the first of January the following year, then two hundred and fifty units would vest every quarter thereafter until the grant is fully vested. Vesting dates are calculated from your grant date, not your start date. Refresh grants, if offered, have their own independent four-year schedules starting from the date of each new grant.

## What Happens on Termination

If you leave the company voluntarily or are terminated before reaching the one-year cliff, all unvested RSUs are forfeited and you receive nothing from that grant. If you leave after the cliff, any RSUs that have already vested are yours to keep. RSUs that have not yet vested at the time of your departure are forfeited without compensation, regardless of the reason for leaving, unless stated otherwise in a separation agreement. In cases of termination due to company restructuring, the company may at its discretion accelerate vesting of a portion of unvested grants, but this is not guaranteed and will be communicated individually. In the event of an acquisition or merger, vesting treatment depends on the terms of the deal and will be communicated to all employees as part of the transaction announcement.

## How to Check Your Grant

You can view your RSU grant details, vesting schedule, and current unvested balance at any time by logging into Carta at carta.com using your company email address. The platform shows each individual grant, the grant date, total units awarded, units vested to date, and the upcoming vesting events. Tax documents related to RSU vestings are also made available through Carta each year. If you have questions about your grant that are not answered in Carta, contact the People Operations team by emailing equity@company.com. For questions about the tax treatment of your vesting shares, you are encouraged to consult a personal tax advisor, as individual circumstances vary.
